Payments that stop short of a terminal state

I connect payment providers and find out why payments hang: webhooks, retries, idempotency, reconciliation. The bugs sit between the provider and your database, not in a particular language or framework, so the stack doesn't matter: the same failures turn up in WooCommerce and Magento, Odoo and Medusa, and in custom backends.

Write to hello@terminalstate.dev — a symptom and one payment id are enough to start.

Paid at the provider, pending on your side?

A payment that sits in pending for hours is almost never stuck at the provider. The money moved, or didn't, within seconds. What is stuck is your knowledge of it: the callback carrying the final status was never applied. Between the provider and the row in your database there are seven places where that happens, and each one has its own check.

Fixed upstream

The same failures, found in open-source payment code and reproduced against each project's own code before they were reported. Of twenty-six reports, eight are fixed, four of them by my own pull requests, and four more are in progress. A few of them:

  • Bagisto · Razorpay: the amount was truncated instead of rounded, so totals like ₹19.99 were charged and no order was placed. Fixed by my PR. PR #11524
  • Paid Memberships Pro · Stripe: the webhook answered 200 when re-fetching the event from Stripe failed, so Stripe never redelivered it. Fixed by my PRs. PR #3890 · PR #3898
  • Adyen for Magento 2 · split payments: orders failed with “Full order amount has not been authorized” because authorised amounts were compared at a different precision than they are stored. Confirmed; Adyen is reviewing my patch. PR #3379
  • Odoo · Mollie: a failed status request to Mollie was acknowledged with 200, so the transaction stayed in draft. Fixed by Odoo. fix · case

How an audit starts

  1. You describe the symptom and send the id of one stuck payment. I reply with which of the seven places it usually is, and what I would need to read.
  2. You export what you can: payments and their status history, inbound webhook logs, delivery attempts. callback-audit runs the first pass on those files, on your side if you prefer.
  3. Within a week: the document, and a walk-through of it.
